The Problem: What RC Costs Us

Regatta Central handles registration for the Brighton Burn, but the overhead is significant for a community fundraising event.

Fee ComponentRate
Platform / admin fee~8% per entry
Credit card processing~3% per transaction
Estimated combined overhead~10–11%

On a $30 entry × 200 participants = ~$600+ to Regatta Central annually.

The Opportunity: Own Our Registration

Addressing Key Concerns

⚠️ Regatta Central Advertising / Visibility RC may de-list the event if we don't use their registration. However:
  • The Brighton Burn draws a local, community audience — not primarily sourced from RC event calendars
  • We can advertise via BRC website, social media, school networks, and rowing community emails
  • We pay RC more in fees than we would ever lose in registrations from reduced visibility
  • Recommendation: Accept the risk. Proceed independently.
⚠️ Insurance
  • The team (BRC) insures the event directly — not through individual USRowing membership
  • USRowing individual waivers are not our mechanism here
  • Confirm current event liability policy covers independently-registered participants
✅ Online Waiver Action Item → Amy Build a digital waiver into the registration form. Amy to review Regatta Central's current waiver language as a starting point and draft a BRC version. Can be implemented as a required checkbox with full waiver text displayed before payment.

Registration Form Requirements

  • Participant info: Name, age, school/team affiliation (optional — see team naming below)
  • Age handicap: Auto-calculate handicap based on age at time of entry; apply to reported times automatically
  • Heat assignment: Assigned or selected at registration
  • Race goal: Participant enters their target time/goal
  • Relay entry:
    • Must consist of 2 males + 2 females
    • Collect all four participant names
    • Custom relay team name
  • Team name (optional): Participants may race under a custom team name instead of their school — important for college athletes (NCAA compliance) and high schoolers eligible for future team awards
  • Waiver acceptance: Required checkbox before payment

Recommended Platform Approach

BRC's site runs on Wix. The path of least resistance:

  • Wix Events + Stripe (nonprofit rate) — native integration, ~2–4 hours to set up, data stored in Wix CRM, auto-confirmation emails
  • OR: Zeffy embed — zero processing fees, embed form into existing event page, slightly less native but fully functional
  • All registrant data exportable to Google Sheets for day-of check-in

Cost: The Wix Site Itself

BRC is likely paying $350–$470/year at standard pricing. A 70% nonprofit discount is available via TechSoup — potentially cutting cost to ~$100–$140 for two years. Check if this has been claimed.
